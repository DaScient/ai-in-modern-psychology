## Overview

Natural-language processing for sentiment analysis, topic modelling, and clinical-text mining.

## 7.1 From Bag-of-Words to Transformers

Text representation has progressed from tf–idf through word embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe) to contextual embeddings from large language models.

## 7.2 Sentiment, Emotion, and Stance

Lexicon-based methods (LIWC) remain useful for theory-driven research; transformer-based classifiers dominate predictive benchmarks.

## 7.3 Clinical Text Mining

De-identified EHR notes can be mined for suicide-related language, medication adherence, and side-effects — with attention to bias from clinician language.

## 7.4 Risks and Limits

LLMs can fabricate, leak training data, and reproduce demographic stereotypes. Their use in clinical settings requires human-in-the-loop review.
