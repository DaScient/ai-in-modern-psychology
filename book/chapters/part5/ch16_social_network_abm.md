## Overview

Agent-based models of polarization, opinion dynamics, and contagion.

## 16.1 Why ABMs?

Agent-based models capture emergent macro-level patterns from heterogeneous micro-level rules — well suited to social-psychological phenomena.

## 16.2 Polarization Dynamics

Bounded-confidence and reinforcing-feedback models reproduce echo chambers and group polarization.

## 16.3 Implementation in Mesa

The Mesa Python framework provides scheduler, space, and visualization abstractions; see `code/social/polarization_abm.py`.

## 16.4 Validation Strategies

Pattern-oriented modelling and indirect calibration link ABM outputs to observed time-series.
