## Overview

Cross-cultural biases in AI systems and the WEIRD-sample problem.

## 18.1 The WEIRD Problem

Psychological theory and training data alike over-sample Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, Democratic populations.

## 18.2 Cross-Cultural Validity

Constructs measured in one cultural context may not transfer. Cultural adaptation is more than translation.

## 18.3 Multilingual NLP

Performance gaps across languages reflect resource imbalance and tokenizer design choices.

## 18.4 Toward Culturally Situated AI

Participatory design with affected communities is necessary, not optional.
