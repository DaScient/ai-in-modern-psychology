## Overview

Social contagion, centrality measures, and influence maximization.

## 17.1 Centrality Measures

Degree, betweenness, closeness, and eigenvector centrality each capture different notions of importance.

## 17.2 Contagion Models

Independent-cascade and linear-threshold models formalise behavioural and informational spread.

## 17.3 Influence Maximization

Submodular optimization yields provable approximation guarantees for selecting seed sets.

## 17.4 Ethical Caveats

Influence techniques shade easily into manipulation; transparency and consent matter.
