# Chapter 14: Adaptive Learning

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

- Understand the key concepts introduced in *Adaptive Learning*.
- Connect chapter content to computational implementations in `/code` and `/notebooks`.
- Identify ethical considerations relevant to this topic.

## Overview

Intelligent tutoring systems, knowledge tracing, and personalization.

## 14.1 Knowledge Tracing

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing and Deep Knowledge Tracing estimate evolving skill mastery from response sequences.

## 14.2 Item Selection

Multi-armed-bandit and IRT-based selection strategies balance learning gain, engagement, and assessment validity.

## 14.3 Student Models vs. Domain Models

Separating *what the learner knows* from *how the domain is structured* improves transfer and explainability.

## 14.4 Equity in Adaptive Learning

Personalization can amplify privilege if not designed with fairness in mind.
