## Overview

Multimodal sensing for workplace well-being, with attention to surveillance concerns.

## 15.1 Multimodal Signals

Calendar density, email sentiment, sleep, and heart-rate variability can jointly predict burnout risk.

## 15.2 Intervention Design

Detection without intervention is surveillance. Personalized nudges, workload re-distribution, and managerial training are necessary complements.

## 15.3 Worker Voice and Consent

Affected workers must have meaningful consent and opt-out options — see the [informed-consent checklist](../../../ethics_toolkit/informed_consent_checklist.md).

## 15.4 Bias Across Roles

Burnout signals differ by role, gender, and culture; uniform thresholds will mis-classify systematically.
