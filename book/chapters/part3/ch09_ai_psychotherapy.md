## Overview

How AI augments — and sometimes replaces — components of psychotherapy.

## 9.1 Augmented Therapy

AI tools support therapists with session summaries, fidelity monitoring, and homework reminders rather than replacing the therapeutic alliance.

## 9.2 Stand-alone Conversational Agents

Woebot, Wysa, and Replika offer scalable but uneven access to CBT-style interventions. Evidence for short-term symptom reduction is promising; long-term outcomes are unclear.

## 9.3 The Alliance Question

Therapeutic alliance accounts for ~30 % of outcome variance. Whether users form a real alliance with a chatbot — and whether that helps — is an open empirical question.
