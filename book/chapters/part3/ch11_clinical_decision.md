## Overview

How AI risk-prediction tools shape — and sometimes distort — clinical judgement.

## 11.1 Decision Support, Not Decision Replacement

AI risk scores should appear as a calibrated probability with an explanation, not as a verdict. Workflow integration determines whether they actually change care.

## 11.2 Risk Communication

Patients struggle with low base-rate probabilities. Frequency framing and visual aids reduce miscalibration.

## 11.3 Automation Bias

Clinicians may defer to the algorithm even when it is wrong. Counter-measures: forced reflection prompts, randomized model-suppression audits.
