## Overview

Real-time risk detection, NLP-based crisis signals, and safety protocols.

## 12.1 Detecting Crisis Language

Pattern-matching plus LLM classifiers can flag suicide-related text. False-negative cost is high; conservative thresholds are essential.

## 12.2 Safety Planning

Stanley–Brown safety planning intervention can be partially automated, but human follow-up remains the gold standard.

## 12.3 Postvention

After an incident, AI tools can help identify at-risk peers and trigger outreach.

## 12.4 Equity Considerations

Crisis-line use varies by language, culture, and access. Models must be audited across demographic groups.
