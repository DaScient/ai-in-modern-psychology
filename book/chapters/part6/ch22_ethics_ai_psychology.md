## Overview

Principled AI: autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, justice — operationalized.

## 22.1 Four Bioethical Principles

Autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, and justice translate to AI through informed consent, benefit assessment, harm audits, and fairness metrics.
