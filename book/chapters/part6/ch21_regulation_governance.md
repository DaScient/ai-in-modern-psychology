## Overview

GDPR, HIPAA, the EU AI Act, and emerging frameworks for psychological AI.

## 21.1 GDPR and Article 22

Article 22 of the GDPR restricts solely automated decisions with significant effects and grants rights of explanation and contest.

## 21.2 HIPAA and Mental-Health Data

HIPAA's privacy and security rules apply to covered entities; many digital-health apps fall outside its scope, leaving gaps.

## 21.3 The EU AI Act

Risk-tiered regulation that classifies many psychological-AI applications as high-risk, with conformity-assessment obligations.

## 21.4 Professional Standards

APA and BPS draft guidelines for AI use in psychological practice are evolving rapidly.
