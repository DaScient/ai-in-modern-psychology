## Overview

Engagement optimization, addiction by design, and mental-health impact.

## 19.1 Engagement vs. Well-Being

Optimising click-through and dwell time can produce content that erodes well-being. Outcomes-aware recommendation is a partial remedy.

## 19.2 Adolescent Mental Health

Evidence linking social-media use to depression and self-harm is mixed but accumulating; causal designs remain rare.

## 19.3 Algorithmic Amplification

Recommender feedback loops can amplify extreme content; counterfactual evaluation is needed to estimate harm.

## 19.4 Design Interventions

Friction prompts, chronological feeds, and content-warning interstitials are evidence-supported levers.
