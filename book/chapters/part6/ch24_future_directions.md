## Overview

AGI speculation, affective computing, neuro-symbolic AI, and what psychologists should prepare for.

## 24.1 Affective Computing

Emotion recognition is over-claimed and under-validated; rigorous psychophysiology constrains realistic applications.

## 24.2 Neuro-Symbolic Hybrids

Combining the data-efficiency of symbolic systems with the perceptual power of neural networks remains a research frontier.

## 24.3 Foundation Models in Psychology

Large multimodal models will increasingly mediate research workflows; reproducibility and provenance become critical.

## 24.4 What Psychologists Should Do Next

Develop computational literacy, contribute to governance, and insist on ethically grounded deployment.
