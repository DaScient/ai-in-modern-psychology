# AI in Modern Psychology

**A Computational Psychology Workbench**

> *"Not a static textbook. A computational psychology workbench where theories become code, and code becomes evidence."*

Welcome to the living companion to **AI in Modern Psychology** (DaScient Press, 2026). This online book transforms the concepts from the print edition into executable models, interactive case studies, and governance toolkits.

## How to Use This Book

- **Read** the chapters below for conceptual grounding.
- **Run** the linked notebooks in Binder (no installation needed).
- **Contribute** improvements via [GitHub](https://github.com/DaScient/ai-in-modern-psychology).

## Book Structure

| Part | Title | Chapters |
|------|-------|---------|
| I | Foundations of AI in Psychology | 1–3 |
| II | AI as Research Tool | 4–7 |
| III | Clinical Applications | 8–12 |
| IV | I/O & Educational Psychology | 13–15 |
| V | Social & Cultural AI | 16–18 |
| VI | Real-World Deployment & Ethics | 19–24 |
