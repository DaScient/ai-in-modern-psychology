# 16-Week Syllabus — AI in Modern Psychology (Undergraduate)

> Maps the book's 24 chapters to a typical 16-week academic semester.
> Each week pairs a chapter (or pair) with a reading, a notebook, and a discussion prompt.

## Course Description

This course introduces psychology majors to the methods, applications, and
ethical implications of artificial intelligence in psychological research and
practice. Students will read the textbook, implement small models in Python,
and complete an applied final project.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course students will be able to:

1. Explain the historical and conceptual relationship between AI and psychology.
2. Implement and critique simple machine-learning models for psychological data.
3. Apply fairness metrics to audit an algorithmic decision system.
4. Articulate ethical and regulatory considerations for AI in clinical practice.
5. Complete a small reproducible research project using these tools.

## Academic Integrity & AI Use

Generative AI tools may be used to learn and to draft, but every submission
must include a brief statement of what AI assistance was used and how. Falsifying
this statement is treated as plagiarism.
