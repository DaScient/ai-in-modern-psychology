# 8-Week Graduate Seminar — AI in Modern Psychology

> Research-focused, paper-driven companion course for graduate students.

## Format

Two 90-minute sessions per week. One paper-led discussion, one hands-on lab.
Students rotate as discussion leaders.

## Schedule

| Week | Theme | Primary readings | Lab |
|------|-------|------------------|-----|
| 1 | Foundations & critique | Chapters 1–3; Marcus (2020); Smolensky (1988) | Set up environment |
| 2 | Digital phenotyping | Chapter 4; Onnela & Rauch (2016) | Sensor pipeline |
| 3 | Memory and cognitive architectures | Chapters 3, 6; Anderson et al. (2004) | ACT-R notebook |
| 4 | Clinical risk prediction | Chapters 8, 11; Walsh et al. (2017) | SHAP audit |
| 5 | Therapy chatbots | Chapters 9, 10, 12; Fitzpatrick et al. (2017) | Safety-protocol design |
| 6 | Algorithmic fairness | Chapters 13, 22; Barocas, Hardt & Narayanan (2019) | Fairness audit |
| 7 | Social-network ABMs | Chapters 16–17; Hegselmann & Krause (2002) | Polarization sim |
| 8 | Governance, futures, student talks | Chapters 21–24; Bender et al. (2021) | Final presentations |

## Deliverables

- Two seminar facilitations.
- One critical-paper review (≤ 1500 words).
- One implementation project with public-facing repository.
