# Informed Consent Checklist for AI-Assisted Studies

> Companion to *AI in Modern Psychology*, Chapter 22.5.

A consent form for AI-assisted psychological studies should explicitly address
each of the following items.

## Disclosure

- [ ] The study uses AI (specify which models, briefly).
- [ ] The purpose of the AI component is described in plain language.
- [ ] The type of data collected (text, audio, video, sensor) is listed.
- [ ] Whether data leaves the device / institution is stated.

## Risks

- [ ] Risks of automated inference are explained (e.g., a model may infer
      sensitive attributes not directly collected).
- [ ] Risks of re-identification are explained.
- [ ] Limits of confidentiality are stated (mandatory reporting, etc.).

## Benefits

- [ ] Direct benefits (if any) are stated honestly — including "none".
- [ ] Indirect benefits to society are described.

## Data Handling

- [ ] Storage location is specified.
- [ ] Retention period is specified.
- [ ] Deletion procedure on request is described.
- [ ] Third-party processors are listed.

## Rights

- [ ] Right to withdraw at any time without penalty.
- [ ] Right to access one's own data.
- [ ] Right to correct or delete data.
- [ ] Right to obtain a human review of any automated decision.

## Comprehension Aids

- [ ] Form is written at a 6th–8th grade reading level.
- [ ] Form is offered in the participant's preferred language.
- [ ] Teach-back or comprehension-check questions are used for high-risk studies.

## Contact

- [ ] PI contact information.
- [ ] IRB / ethics-committee contact information.
- [ ] Independent point of contact for AI-specific concerns.

---

A signed copy is provided to the participant.
